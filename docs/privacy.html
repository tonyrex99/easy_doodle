Privacy Policy for Easy Doodle

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Version 1.0

At Easy Doodle (developed by Tony Rex), we believe that your creative drawings, sketches, and notes belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy details our strict commitment to privacy, data security, and compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information Collection & Storage (100% Offline-First)

Easy Doodle is an offline-first application.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is Collected: We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use Easy Doodle.
  • No Tracking or Analytics SDKs: Easy Doodle contains zero advertising tracking networks, zero behavioral analytics trackers, and zero third-party profiling trackers.

2. Device Permissions & Purpose

Easy Doodle requests the minimum permissions necessary to deliver freehand drawing, file export, and photo saving features:

  • Photos & Media (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Storage): Used solely when you explicitly tap "Save to Photos" or "Save Area Grab" to save your finished drawing to your device's Camera Roll / DCIM gallery. On Android 13+, it requests scoped media photo access.
  • Document Access (File System): Used when you explicitly choose to export or import a `.doodle` vector file. Easy Doodle only accesses the specific file you pick via the system document picker.
  • Network Access (`INTERNET`): Included by the underlying mobile runtime framework. No drawings, images, or metadata are ever transmitted over the internet to developer servers.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your drawings or information to any external third party.

When you choose to share a drawing or `.doodle` file via the app's "Share" feature, your device's native Android Share Sheet is invoked. Your drawing is sent directly and exclusively to the application or recipient you select (e.g. WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All doodles, version history snapshots, and auto-recovery drafts are stored strictly inside your device's secure local app storage sandbox.

  • You can delete individual drawings or version milestones at any time using the in-app delete button.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored project files and caches.

5. Children's Privacy

Easy Doodle is suitable for all ages (including children under 13). Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we are in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
