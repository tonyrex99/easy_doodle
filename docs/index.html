Easy Doodle is an intuitive, offline-first freehand drawing app inspired by classic MS Paint with modern vector graphics, selective area screen grab, canvas grid overlays, multi-version history, and auto-recovery.

📥 Download APK (v1.0.0) ⭐ View on GitHub
✏️

Fluid Vector Drawing

Smooth quadratic Bezier curves for pen, artistic brush, highlighter, eraser, and geometric shapes (rectangles, circles, lines, arrows).

✂️

Selective Area Screen Grab

Easily crop and snip any region of your drawing with the resizable viewfinder or "Drag to Snip" freehand box, then share or save instantly.

🌿

Version History & Auto-Recovery

Save milestones as date-stamped versions and revert anytime. Microsoft Word-style auto-recovery safeguards unsaved work if interrupted.

📐

Grid & Paper Backgrounds

Graph grid, dot grid, ruled lines, and isometric patterns with adjustable density, plus paper cream, chalkboard, blueprint, and mint backgrounds.

🔍

Pinch Zoom & Pan

Smooth two-finger pinch to zoom from 40% to 450% and pan around your sheet. Movement takes precedence over drawing so you never make accidental marks.

📁

Native .doodle Files & Thumbnails

Save and share native vector `.doodle` files. Android associates the file type automatically, displaying live vector thumbnails in your library.

Google Play Store & Data Safety Commitment

Easy Doodle is developed following strict Google Play Developer Policies and COPPA guidelines:

  • No Personal Data Collected: No accounts, no advertising identifiers, and no user tracking.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: Your drawings remain on your phone or tablet in private app sandbox storage.
  • Target SDK 36: Fully compatible with the newest Android 15 & 16 security guidelines, 64-bit architectures, and scoped storage rules.
  • Safe for Everyone: Rated PEGI 3 / Everyone — no ads, in-app purchases, or external tracking.
